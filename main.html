Summary

Inception is a science fiction thriller directed by Christopher Nolan. The story follows Dom Cobb, a skilled thief who steals secrets through dream-sharing technology. He is given a chance to erase his criminal history by planting an idea into someone's mind.

Actors

The movie stars Leonardo DiCaprio as Dom Cobb, Joseph Gordon-Levitt as Arthur, and Ellen Page as Ariadne.
