Summary

Inception is a science fiction movie directed by Christopher Nolan. The story follows Dom Cobb, a skilled thief who enters people's dreams to steal secrets. He is offered a chance to clear his criminal history by performing the difficult task of planting an idea into someone's mind.

Actors

Leonardo DiCaprio played Dom Cobb, Joseph Gordon-Levitt played Arthur, and Elliot Page played Ariadne.
